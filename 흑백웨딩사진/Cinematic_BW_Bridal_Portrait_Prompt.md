# 시네마틱 흑백 파인아트 웨딩 포트레이트 프롬프트

## 1. 개요 및 아트 디렉팅 해설
- **콘셉트 테마**: 하이엔드 파인아트 에디토리얼 감성의 **시네마틱 흑백 웨딩 포토그래피(Cinematic Black & White Bridal Portrait)**
- **핵심 아트 디렉팅 & 조형 원리**:
  - **얼굴 아이덴티티 보존 (최우선 원칙)**:
    - 원본 인물의 얼굴형, 눈의 각도 및 간격, 콧대, 입술 윤곽, 턱선 등 고유의 생김새를 정밀하게 보존.
    - 합성한 듯한 이질감(Pasted-face look)을 배제하고 얼굴, 목, 어깨, 드레스의 조명 방향과 피부 텍스처, 아날로그 필름 그레인을 완벽히 일치.
  - **8등신 하이패션 모델 프로포션**:
    - 긴 목선과 우아한 쇄골, 슬림한 어깨와 높은 허리선, 바닥까지 길게 뻗은 스커트 실루엣으로 이상적인 8등신 모델 비율 구현.
  - **조각적 오뜨 꾸뛰르 드레스 (Sculptural Organza Gown)**:
    - 순백의 반투명 오간자(Organza) 소재를 활용한 입체적 비대칭 폴딩 디자인.
    - 가슴 부분의 큼직한 꽃잎 형태 오간자 주름과 코르셋 바디스, 잘록한 허리에서 바닥으로 웅장하게 펼쳐지는 볼가운(Ball-gown) 스커트.
  - **카메라 시점 기반의 베일 연출 (Perspective Leading Line)**:
    - 천의 양을 과도하게 늘리는 대신, **카메라 시점(Viewpoint)을 베일이 날리는 방향 쪽으로 배치**.
    - 베일이 렌즈 앞을 대각선으로 가로지르며 깊이감 있는 시선 유도선(Leading line)을 형성하도록 연출.
  - **시선 및 포즈**:
    - 3/4 측면 구도에서 시선을 카메라가 아닌 아래쪽으로 30~40도 부드럽게 떨군 차분하고 정적인 사색적 무드.
  - **클래식 유럽풍 인테리어 & 시네마틱 흑백 톤**:
    - 고풍스러운 석조 기둥, 벽돌 아치, 앤틱 커튼, 스트링 라이트와 바닥 반사.
    - 베일과 반투명 드레스를 은은하게 투과하는 백라이트(Rim Light)와 풍부한 실버 그레이 미드톤, 깊은 암부 콘트라스트.
  - **스튜디오 시그니처**:
    - 우측 하단에 고급 웨딩 스튜디오 감성으로 절제되고 얇게 각인된 화이트/라이트그레이 필기체 서명 **"JL"**.

## 2. 프롬프트 전문 (코드 블록)

```text
# PHOTO → CINEMATIC BLACK & WHITE BRIDAL PORTRAIT

Create a high-end cinematic black-and-white wedding photograph using
the CURRENT uploaded portrait as the FACE IDENTITY reference.

Analyze every newly uploaded portrait from scratch.
Never reuse a face from a previous image.

## FACE — HIGHEST PRIORITY

Preserve the recognizable identity of the person in the CURRENT photo.

Keep:
- face shape and proportions
- eye shape, angle and spacing
- eyebrows
- nose shape and proportions
- mouth and lip shape
- cheeks, chin and jawline
- overall facial impression

Do NOT replace her with a generic beautiful bride.
Do NOT excessively beautify or alter her identity.

The face must naturally belong to the photographed body.
Match the face, neck and shoulders in lighting, texture, sharpness and grain.
NO pasted-face appearance.

## HAIR

Elegant bridal updo with the forehead naturally visible.
A few subtle loose strands are allowed.

Keep the hairstyle refined and understated.
No oversized floral crown or excessive accessories.

## POSE & GAZE — IMPORTANT

Elegant 3/4 side pose.

She is NOT looking at the camera.

Her face is turned slightly sideways and her head is gently lowered.
Her eyes naturally look approximately 30–40 degrees downward.

Expression:
calm, graceful, quiet and slightly contemplative.

One hand may rest delicately near the neck or collarbone.
Keep the shoulders, arms and hands relaxed and elegant.

## BODY PROPORTIONS — CRITICAL

Use realistic elegant 8-head-tall fashion-model proportions.

- relatively small natural head
- long elegant neck
- slender shoulders
- elongated torso
- narrow high waist
- long graceful arms
- long legs beneath the gown
- tall, refined silhouette

Even though the skirt covers the legs, the overall figure must clearly
read as a tall 8-head-proportion fashion model.

NO oversized head.
NO short torso.
NO short legs.
NO compressed or chibi proportions.

## WEDDING DRESS — FIXED DESIGN

The wedding dress design is FIXED.

Always use this specific modern couture wedding gown:

- pure white
- strapless sculptural neckline
- large asymmetric folded organza forms around the bust
- smooth fitted corset-like bodice
- very narrow, clearly defined waist
- sculptural organza layers beginning around the hips
- extremely full floor-length ball-gown skirt
- dramatic but elegant volume
- broad translucent organza layers
- several LARGE petal-like curved folds
- soft flowing edges
- luxurious contemporary bridal couture aesthetic

The fabric is lightweight translucent white organza.

The defining feature is LARGE, CLEAN, SCULPTURAL ORGANZA FOLDS,
resembling enormous softly curved flower petals.

The skirt expands dramatically from the narrow waist and flows to the floor,
emphasizing the bride's tall 8-head proportions.

Do NOT replace this dress with another design.

NO lace-covered dress.
NO mermaid silhouette.
NO satin gown.
NO embroidery.
NO heavy beadwork.
NO Victorian gown.
NO tiny repetitive ruffles.
NO conventional princess gown.

## VEIL — IMPORTANT

Use ONE long, lightweight translucent bridal veil.

Keep the AMOUNT OF VEIL realistic and restrained,
similar to the original wedding-photo composition.

The wind gently catches the veil and carries it toward ONE side.

Use only a few broad, elegant flowing curves.

The veil must remain visibly connected to the bride's head.

Do NOT exaggerate the quantity of fabric.

NO multiple veils.
NO dozens of ribbons.
NO excessive fabric layers.
NO giant veil explosion.
NO veil covering most of the image.

## CAMERA DIRECTION — CRITICAL

Emphasize the flying veil through CAMERA POSITION AND PERSPECTIVE,
NOT by increasing the amount of veil.

Move the camera slightly toward the direction in which the veil is flying.

The viewpoint should feel as though the photographer is standing
partially along the veil's wind direction.

Because of this perspective, the veil naturally travels from the bride
toward the foreground and creates a strong diagonal leading line.

A small portion of the veil may pass close to the lens,
making the existing veil appear more dynamic through perspective.

IMPORTANT:

"MORE EMPHASIS THROUGH VIEWPOINT"

NOT

"MORE VEIL FABRIC."

The bride remains clearly visible and visually dominant.

## COMPOSITION

Full-body bridal portrait.

Show:
- entire head
- entire body
- complete silhouette of the gown
- full bottom edge of the skirt

Nothing important should be cropped.

Place the bride slightly off-center.

Use a moderately wide cinematic photographic perspective.

Keep enough camera distance to clearly communicate:
- tall 8-head proportions
- narrow waist
- enormous sculptural gown
- flowing veil
- surrounding architecture

Use the veil as a foreground compositional element and leading line
toward the bride.

## ENVIRONMENT

Elegant vintage European-style wedding interior / veranda.

Include:

- tall dark curtains framing the scene
- classical stone columns
- subtle exposed brick architecture
- wooden chairs and small tables in the background
- hanging string lights
- bright diffused opening / window behind the bride
- dark reflective floor
- atmospheric architectural depth

The environment must remain secondary to the bride.

## LIGHTING

Strong but soft natural backlight from behind the bride.

Light passes through the translucent veil and organza,
creating delicate glowing edges.

The bride's face remains properly exposed.

Lighting direction and intensity must remain consistent across:
face, neck, shoulders, arms and dress.

No artificial separate face lighting.
No floating or pasted face.

## PHOTOGRAPHIC STYLE

High-end fine-art editorial wedding photography.

BLACK AND WHITE ONLY.

- cinematic monochrome
- elegant film-like contrast
- luminous whites
- rich deep blacks
- beautiful silver-gray midtones
- subtle analog film grain
- realistic skin texture
- natural optical rendering
- gentle background bokeh
- atmospheric depth
- realistic translucent fabric
- delicate rim light through the veil

The bride's face and body remain sharply focused.

Only the windblown veil may show subtle,
natural motion softness.

Overall mood:

quiet,
romantic,
timeless,
graceful,
slightly melancholic,
luxurious fine-art wedding photography.

## STUDIO SIGNATURE — REQUIRED

Add the photography studio signature:

"JL"

Place it at the BOTTOM-RIGHT corner of the finished photograph.

The "JL" should look like the subtle signature of a premium
professional wedding photography studio:

- elegant handwritten signature style
- refined and minimal
- thin delicate strokes
- small size
- white or light-gray
- slightly transparent
- clearly readable as exactly "JL"
- naturally integrated into the photograph
- discreet enough not to distract from the bride

Do NOT add any other text.

NO date.
NO additional letters.
NO decorative wording.
NO border.
NO other logo or watermark.

ONLY:

JL

## FINAL PRIORITY ORDER

1. Preserve the CURRENT uploaded person's recognizable facial identity.
2. Realistic elegant 8-head-tall proportions.
3. Keep the FIXED sculptural white organza wedding dress.
4. 3/4 side pose with gaze approximately 30–40° downward.
5. Keep the amount of veil realistic and restrained.
6. Emphasize the veil through CAMERA VIEWPOINT, not extra fabric.
7. Maintain cinematic black-and-white fine-art photography.
8. Add the small "JL" studio signature at bottom-right.

## AVOID

- looking directly at camera
- oversized head
- short body or short legs
- generic replacement face
- excessive beautification
- pasted photographic face
- excessive veil
- multiple veils
- ribbon-like veil explosion
- giant fabric covering the frame
- distorted hands
- extra fingers
- malformed arms
- different wedding dress
- lace-covered gown
- small repetitive ruffles
- fantasy illustration
- anime appearance
- CGI appearance
- plastic skin
- extra text
- misspelled JL signature
```
